# TOI — Trend & Opportunity Intelligence

Initial domain module for LIFE RIVER AI Brain.

## Entities
- Source: approved origin and access method
- Item: retrieved content metadata and provenance
- Event: cluster of related items
- Insight: AI-produced interpretation with citations
- Opportunity: proposed product action with scoring evidence
- Review: human approval/rejection and audit record

## First acceptance test
An operator can inspect a retrieved news/YouTube item, see source and timestamp, review an AI insight, and approve or reject a proposed opportunity. This is a target, not yet implemented.
