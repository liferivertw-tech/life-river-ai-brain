# LIFE RIVER AI Brain

Shared intelligence foundation for LIFE RIVER SYSTEM.

## First module: TOI
Trend & Opportunity Intelligence collects permitted news, video, search and social signals; verifies provenance; analyzes emerging needs; and proposes opportunities for human review.

## Principles
- Source-first, timestamped, traceable evidence.
- Use official APIs, RSS and authorized data access; respect privacy and platform terms.
- Human approval before publishing, creating issues, or modifying other product systems.
- Product repositories remain independent; integration via versioned APIs.
- No automatic cross-product data access without explicit permission.
